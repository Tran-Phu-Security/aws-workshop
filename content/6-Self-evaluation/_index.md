### Areas for Improvement

- **Discipline:** Improve time management and maintain a more consistent learning schedule to ensure steady progress.
- **Problem-Solving Skills:** Strengthen analytical thinking and develop the ability to troubleshoot technical issues more independently.
- **Communication:** Improve confidence and clarity when explaining technical concepts, discussing solutions, and presenting learning outcomes.

### Additional Questions

**1. What were you most satisfied with during the program?**

What satisfied me the most was the opportunity to explore and practice with a wide range of AWS services and cloud-related technologies. Working with services such as EC2, VPC, S3, RDS, IAM, and AWS Lambda helped me better understand how cloud infrastructure operates and how different components integrate with one another. Additionally, learning about Infrastructure as Code, system monitoring, automation, containerization, and CI/CD broadened my knowledge beyond fundamental cloud computing concepts.

**2. What do you think the program should improve to better support future participants?**

I believe future participants would benefit even more if the program provided a clearer, structured learning roadmap accompanied by specific milestones and recommended reference materials for each topic. Incorporating more practical labs and real-world technical scenarios would also help learners reinforce their understanding of AWS services and build stronger troubleshooting capabilities. Furthermore, regular review and feedback sessions would help participants identify knowledge gaps and improve their learning pace.

**3. Would you recommend this program to other students? Why?**

Yes, I would readily recommend FCAJ to students interested in cloud computing and AWS technologies. The program offers valuable opportunities to explore numerous cloud services, gain practical hands-on experience, and develop essential technical skills. Moreover, it fosters self-directed learning and helps participants familiarize themselves with technologies and methodologies widely used in modern IT environments.

### Suggestions & Expectations

To further enhance the learning experience, I would like to propose the following suggestions:

- **Structured Learning Roadmap:** Establish a clearer curriculum roadmap outlining prerequisite foundational knowledge and recommended documentation for each technical topic.
- **More Integrated Hands-on Labs:** Add more comprehensive labs and practical exercises that combine multiple AWS services so learners can better understand end-to-end system interactions.
- **Troubleshooting Workshops:** Organize dedicated sessions focused on identifying errors, performing root cause analysis, and resolving common cloud infrastructure issues.
- **Regular Progress Feedback:** Provide more frequent feedback on practical assignments so participants can track their progress and identify areas needing improvement.
- **Knowledge-Sharing Opportunities:** Encourage technical discussions and experience-sharing sessions between participants and mentors to strengthen communication skills and collaborative learning.

I hope the program will continue creating opportunities for participants to explore advanced AWS technologies, reinforce practical skills, and deepen their understanding of cloud computing. Personally, I also aim to keep improving my technical problem-solving abilities, communication skills, and study discipline through continuous practice and feedback.

Finally, I would like to express my sincere gratitude for the opportunity to participate in FCAJ and for the support provided throughout the program. The knowledge and experience I have gained have helped me build a stronger technical foundation and establish a clearer direction for my future studies and professional development.
